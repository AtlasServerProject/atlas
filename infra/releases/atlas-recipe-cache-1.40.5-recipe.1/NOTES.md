Tag-based crafting fix: the Bottle Cap recipe filter read every ingredient while recipes load, before item tags are bound. Ingredient caches that list forever, so tag ingredients (planks, logs, stone, wool...) matched nothing and recipes such as oak planks, sticks and crafting tables produced no result since Core 1.40.0. The filter now clears the cache after reading, so vanilla rebuilds it with bound tags. Bottle Cap detection is unchanged. Only RestrictedRecipeMixin, the new IngredientCacheAccessor and the mixin list differ from the installed lobby.2 release; undeployed hub-inventory changes from the workspace are not included. No world/database edits.
